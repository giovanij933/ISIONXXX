MY AFFIRMATION // SEVEN PRACTICES. ONE ME.

I am what I
practice.
Every day.

Seven promises to myself, one for each part of the life I’m building. Read slowly. Mean every line.

  1. 01 / TODAY

    I begin where I am. I check in, make a plan, and take one step.

  2. 02 / MIND

    I make room for stillness. I pause, I breathe, and I begin again.

  3. 03 / TRAINING

    I show up for my body. Session by session, I am getting stronger.

  4. 04 / NUTRITION

    I feed the life I’m building. I choose food that gives me energy.

  5. 05 / LEARN

    I stay curious. Lesson by lesson, I turn what I learn into skill.

  6. 06 / JOURNAL

    I trade with patience and a plan. I record every decision and learn from it, win or lose.

  7. 07 / REWIRE

    An urge is a wave, not an order. I notice it, let it pass, and choose what serves me.

I am a work in progress, and that’s the point. I don’t need a perfect day. I only need to come back, one day at a time.
